                    THE VELVET ROOM

This room exists between mind and matter, between dream and
reality. Its master keeps the compendium of every Persona ever
fused here, and the ledger of every contract signed.

A guest may stand in this room. A guest may not read this page.

If you are seeing these words, your uid is 0 -- and the kernel
checked before it handed them over, against a mode stored in the
tar header. Try again as `guest` and it will refuse.
